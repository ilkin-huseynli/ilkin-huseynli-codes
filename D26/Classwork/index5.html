Football is the most popular sport in the world. It is played by two teams with eleven players on each side. The main goal of the game is to score by putting the ball into the opponent’s net. Football matches usually last 90 minutes and are divided into two halves. The sport is played in almost every country and has millions of fans. The biggest international football competition is the FIFA World Cup. Many famous clubs like Real Madrid CF and FC Barcelona are known all over the world. Football requires teamwork, skill, speed, and strategy. Major leagues such as the Premier League and La Liga attract millions of viewers every season. Because of its excitement and global popularity, football is often called the beautiful game.
