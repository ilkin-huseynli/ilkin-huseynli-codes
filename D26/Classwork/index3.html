1️⃣ Premier League
Country: United Kingdom
Founded: 1992
Number of Teams: 20
Current Champion: Manchester City FC
Description: The Premier League is one of the most popular football leagues in the world.
It features 20 top clubs from England and is known for its fast, competitive matches and global audience.

2️⃣ La Liga
Country: Spain
Founded: 1929
Number of Teams: 20
Famous Clubs: Real Madrid CF, FC Barcelona
Description: La Liga is the top professional football league in Spain.
It is famous for its technical style of play and world-class clubs like Real Madrid and Barcelona.
